Park lab is excited to welcome BIG PhD student Daniel Feng to the team! Daniel graduated from the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign in 2025 with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. As an undergraduate, he researched computational cancer phylogenetics, network clustering, and the probabilistic modelling of errors in microsatellite duplex sequencing. In the Park Lab, Daniel is developing algorithmic methods for analyzing and compressing different types of next generation sequencing data! Welcome Daniel!
