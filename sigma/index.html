HRD Detection for Gene Panels

The SigMA algorithm detects homologous recombination deficiency (HRD) by identifying mutational Signature 3 from panel, exome, or genome data. It uses a likelihood-based tumor subtype model and a multivariate analysis to improve detection sensitivity, especially for low mutation count samples.

Developing a Biomarker for Enhanced PARP Inhibition

SigMA predicts Poly ADP-ribose polymerase (PARP) inhibitor efficiacy up to 66% better than BRCA1/2 mutation-based approaches in breast and ovarian cancers. Read the paper →

Current clinical HRD tests are limited to two copy number-based assays, both approved exclusively for ovarian cancer. SigMA detects the HRD-related Signature 3 using small targeted sequencing panels, requiring significantly fewer mutations than whole-genome or whole-exome approaches.

Access & Citation

SigMA is free to use for academic and non-commercial research purposes. Commercial use of any kind requires a license.
